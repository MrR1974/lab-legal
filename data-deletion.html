User Data Deletion

Superfriends CIAM · Last updated 29 September 2026

Superfriends CIAM keeps only the basic profile information from your sign-in: your name, email address and account identifier. This page explains how to have that data deleted.

1. Remove the lab from Facebook

  1. In Facebook, open Settings & privacy → Settings.
  2. Go to Apps and Websites.
  3. Find Superfriends CIAM and choose Remove.

This stops Facebook from sharing any more information with the lab.

2. Ask for your lab account to be deleted

Open a data deletion request on the lab's GitHub repository. Give the name you signed in with, but do not post your email address or any other personal details in the issue. The lab owner will delete your account from the Superfriends CIAM tenant within 30 days and confirm on the issue.

What is deleted

Your user account in the Superfriends CIAM tenant is deleted, along with every profile attribute stored with it. The test applications connected to the tenant keep no other copy of your data.

Superfriends CIAM is a personal Microsoft Entra External ID test lab. It is not a commercial service.
