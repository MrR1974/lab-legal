User Data Deletion

Entra External ID OIDC Lab · Last updated 29 September 2026

Entra External ID OIDC Lab keeps only the basic profile information from your sign-in: your name, email address and account identifier. This page explains how to have that data deleted.

1. Remove the lab from Facebook

  1. In Facebook, open Settings & privacy → Settings.
  2. Go to Apps and Websites.
  3. Find Entra External ID OIDC Lab and choose Remove.

This stops Facebook from sharing any more information with the lab.

2. Ask for your lab account to be deleted

Open a data deletion request on the lab's GitHub repository. Give the name you signed in with, but do not post your email address or any other personal details in the issue. The lab owner will delete your account from the Entra External ID tenant within 30 days and confirm on the issue.

What is deleted

Your user account in the lab's Entra External ID tenant is deleted, along with every profile attribute stored with it. The lab app keeps no other copy of your data.

Entra External ID OIDC Lab is a personal test lab. It is not a commercial service.
