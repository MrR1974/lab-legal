Privacy Policy

Superfriends CIAM · Last updated 29 September 2026

This policy explains what information Superfriends CIAM ("the lab") receives when you sign in and how that information is used. The lab is a personal Microsoft Entra External ID tenant used to test social sign-in integration. It is not a commercial service.

Information we receive

When you sign in with Facebook or another social identity provider, the lab receives the basic profile information you agree to share. This is normally your name, email address and a unique account identifier. The lab does not ask for your friends list, posts, photos or any other Facebook data.

How the information is used

The information is used only to sign you in and to show the claims in your sign-in token, so social sign-in with Entra External ID can be tested. It is not used for advertising, profiling or marketing, and it is never sold.

Where it is stored

Your account is stored in the Superfriends CIAM tenant, a Microsoft Entra External ID tenant that the lab owner controls. Microsoft processes that data under its own terms and privacy statement. The test applications connected to the tenant keep no database. They hold your sign-in only in a session cookie until you sign out or the session ends.

Sharing

Your information is not shared with anyone else, except with Microsoft as the identity platform and with the social identity provider you chose, such as Facebook.

Retention

Test accounts are deleted when they are no longer needed for testing, or sooner if you ask. See User Data Deletion.

Your choices

You can remove the lab's access at any time in Facebook under Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites. You can also ask for your data to be deleted as described on the User Data Deletion page.

Contact

For questions about this policy, open an issue on the lab's GitHub repository.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.

Superfriends CIAM is a personal Microsoft Entra External ID test lab. It is not a commercial service.
