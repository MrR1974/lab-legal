Privacy Policy

Entra External ID OIDC Lab · Last updated 29 September 2026

This policy explains what information Entra External ID OIDC Lab ("the lab") receives when you sign in and how that information is used. The lab is a personal test environment for identity integration and is not a commercial service.

Information we receive

When you sign in with Facebook or another identity provider, the lab receives the basic profile information you agree to share. This is normally your name, email address and a unique account identifier. The lab does not ask for your friends list, posts, photos or any other Facebook data.

How the information is used

The information is used only to sign you in and to show the claims in your sign-in token, so the identity integration can be tested. It is not used for advertising, profiling or marketing, and it is never sold.

Where it is stored

Your account is stored in a Microsoft Entra External ID tenant that the lab owner controls. Microsoft processes that data under its own terms and privacy statement. The lab app itself keeps no database. It holds your sign-in only in a session cookie until you sign out or the session ends.

Sharing

Your information is not shared with anyone else, except with Microsoft as the identity platform and with Facebook as the identity provider you chose.

Retention

Test accounts are deleted when they are no longer needed for testing, or sooner if you ask. See User Data Deletion.

Your choices

You can remove the lab's access at any time in Facebook under Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites. You can also ask for your data to be deleted as described on the User Data Deletion page.

Contact

For questions about this policy, open an issue on the lab's GitHub repository.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.

Entra External ID OIDC Lab is a personal test lab. It is not a commercial service.
